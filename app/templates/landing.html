Institutional Background

A Legacy of Learning Since 1980

Established in 1980 under the aegis of Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS), PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya No. 1, Ordnance Factory, Itarsi is a distinguished co-educational institution situated in the tranquil, verdant residential estate of the Ordnance Factory, approximately 13 kilometres from Itarsi Junction railway station.

As a recognized PM SHRI (Prime Minister Schools for Rising India) exemplar institution under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, the Vidyalaya spearheads the pedagogical goals of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. Our learning environment fosters critical inquiry, cognitive growth, digital literacy, and deep ethical values for students across all classes.
